Privacy Policy & Data Principles

Refundly stores all refund records locally on your device using HTML5 LocalStorage. We do not require bank logins, passwords, or credit card numbers. Your financial record details stay in your control on your web browser.

Terms of Service & Disclaimer

Refundly is an independent record tracking and follow-up organizer. Refundly does not directly issue payments or process bank transactions.
